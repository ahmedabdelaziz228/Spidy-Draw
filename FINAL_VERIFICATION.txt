───────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Location: gcode_executor.cpp, prepareQueueFit() method
Impact:   Drawings not scaled to fit workspace bounds

Solution Implemented:
───────────────────
✅ Created GCodeValidator class (gcode_validator.h)
✅ Implemented calculateFitTransform() method
✅ Added proper scaling formula:

✅ Added safety margin calculation
✅ Integrated validator into gcode_executor constructor

✅ Removed hardcoded fitScale = 1.0f
